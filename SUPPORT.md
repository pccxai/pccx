# Support

PCCX is an open-source project initiated and operated by Altifigence.
This repository is the specification and documentation hub.

## Where to ask

- Bugs and documentation changes: [PCCX issues](https://github.com/pccxai/pccx/issues).
  Use the RTL or board integration repository for implementation-specific issues.
- Design and reproduction questions: [PCCX Discussions](https://github.com/pccxai/pccx/discussions).
- Security reports: follow [SECURITY.md](https://github.com/pccxai/pccx/blob/main/SECURITY.md).
- Operating, rights and sponsorship enquiries: <contact@altifigence.com>.
- Decisions, funding and asset rights: [Transparency](https://pccx.ai/en/legal/transparency/).
- Altifigence products and services: [Altifigence](https://altifigence.com/).

## Developer support

The **Altifigence Open Source Program** is in preparation. Its proposed
feature access and metered cloud/AI credits are separate benefits; selection,
limits, duration and renewal will be published at launch. This page does not
announce an open application process or guarantee benefits.

Participation in PCCX is possible without buying Altifigence products or
receiving support. Other tools and critical technical feedback are welcome.

## Scope

Community support is best-effort. This page is not a commercial support
contract or service-level guarantee. Commercial services, if separately
agreed, are provided by Altifigence under their own terms. Draft or legacy
offering names in `docs/commercial/` do not establish available services.
