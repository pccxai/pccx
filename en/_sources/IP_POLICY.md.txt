# PCCX IP policy

Updated September 27, 2026. Operational guidance; proposed agreements and
unadopted procedures remain drafts subject to legal review.

## Operator and controlling notices

PCCX is initiated and operated by Altifigence as a company-led open-source
project and community. It is not currently a separate nonprofit foundation.
See [Transparency](https://pccx.ai/en/Transparency/) for governance, commercial
interests, funding and the planned developer support program.

This page does not grant or remove rights. [LICENSE](LICENSE),
[LICENSE-CODE](LICENSE-CODE), file-level notices and executed agreements
control the assets they cover. Publication is not the same as an open-source
license or dedication to the public domain.

## Public and commercial boundaries

| Material | Applicable boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Source code in this repository | Apache-2.0 unless a file states otherwise; preserve notices and applicable obligations. |
| RTL and board integration in separate repositories | Check that repository and individual file notices. |
| Documentation, prose, diagrams and educational material | Publicly readable does not mean freely relicensed; existing documentation permissions apply. |
| PCCX names, logos and trademarks | Separate trademark policy; the code license does not grant brand endorsement. |
| DDS, ALcad and other Altifigence commercial products | Separate product terms; incorporation of open code must comply with its license. |
| Confidential or customer material | Do not publish without the necessary rights and authorization. |

Open-source license rights apply equally to competitors. A commercial tool
purchase is not required to participate in PCCX. No contribution is presumed
to assign its author's rights to Altifigence. Third-party rights, licenses
and authorship records must be preserved.

Before publication, review authority to disclose and any patent or
confidentiality implications. This page makes no patent-validity or
trade-secret determination. The existing filing records remain separate.

## Contributions and support

Follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/pccxai/pccx/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md)
and the applicable repository terms. [DCO.md](DCO.md) and the CLA placeholder
remain informational drafts until formally adopted; this update does not
introduce a sign-off or assignment requirement.

Participation alone does not promise equity, royalties, revenue share,
employment, compensation or commercial support. It does not take away
existing authorship, ownership or license rights. Any additional benefit
requires its applicable published terms or a separate agreement.
