# PCCX Transparency

Operating principles · Updated September 27, 2026

Public versions: [English](https://pccx.ai/en/legal/transparency/) ·
[한국어](https://pccx.ai/ko-kr/legal/transparency/).

## Operator and current structure

PCCX is an open-source semiconductor project and community initiated,
operated and supported by **Altifigence**. It is currently company-led;
it is not a separate independent nonprofit foundation or an organization
recognized by the US IRS under 501(c)(3) or 501(c)(6).

PCCX develops NPU and RTL designs, testbenches, verification examples,
scripts, technical documentation and learning resources where the project
has the right to publish them. It aims to be useful without buying
Altifigence products. Public visibility does not give every asset the same
license; the rights section below explains that boundary.

Altifigence is responsible for operations, budgets and contracts. Its
separate products and services include Digital Design Studio (DDS), ALcad,
development automation, infrastructure and commercial support.

## Decisions and commercial interests

Altifigence has final responsibility for operating direction, budgets and
contracts. Repository maintainers review technical changes according to
the applicable contribution guide. Significant proposals and reasons for
changes should be recorded in public issues, discussions or pull requests.
Changes to maintainers or decision procedures should be explained publicly.

Altifigence seeks both a useful PCCX ecosystem and wider use and revenue
for its commercial tools. The operating direction is to reinvest product
revenue and feedback into PCCX and product development. This is not a
promise of a fixed revenue share, allocation or investment return.

Basic workflows should be documented independently of commercial tools.
Convenience integrations may be added when ready. Paid purchases, positive
reviews and use of a particular tool are not conditions of participation.

## Asset rights and contributions

[LICENSE](LICENSE) and [LICENSE-CODE](LICENSE-CODE), together with narrower
file notices, govern this repository. Source code is Apache-2.0 unless a
file states otherwise. Documentation, prose, diagrams, logos and trademarks
are not covered by that repository-level code license; their existing
permissions and rights notices remain in force. Other repositories have
their own notices.

Existing code and outside contributions are not presumed to become
Altifigence property merely by being included in PCCX. Contributors retain
their applicable rights unless a separate agreement says otherwise.
Submit only work you have authority to provide, and preserve third-party
notices. See [CONTRIBUTING.md](https://github.com/pccxai/pccx/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).

Open-source code may be modified, redistributed and used commercially,
including by competitors, subject to its license. Separate commercial
products remain subject to their own terms and must respect the licenses
of any incorporated public code. Draft DCO or CLA documents do not create
new contributor obligations before formal adoption.

## Funding and external support

The initial operating model uses Altifigence's development and community
budget for maintenance, public technology, documentation, infrastructure
and contributor support. Before introducing outside support or sponsorship,
we will disclose the recipient, use, conditions and sponsor benefits.
Service fees, sponsorship and project support will be identified clearly.

PCCX support is not presented as a tax-deductible contribution to a US
501(c)(3) organization. A future legal or tax status will be announced only
with verifiable information and its applicable scope.

## Altifigence Open Source Program

**In preparation; applications and benefits are not announced as available.**
The proposed program starts with PCCX and may expand as capacity allows.
It envisages time-limited trials for workshop participants, feature access
and capped cloud/AI credits for meaningful contributors, and longer-term
support for sustained maintainers.

Feature access and metered credits will be managed separately. Eligibility,
selection, duration, monthly credit limits and renewal conditions will be
published at launch. Support will not require positive reviews or paid
purchases; contributors may use other tools or decline support.

## Possible future foundation

A separate foundation or shared organization may be considered when public
education/research needs independent governance and funding, or when
multiple companies need to address common industry problems. No transition,
incorporation or tax-exempt status is claimed today. Asset inventories,
licenses, contribution records and operating rules should support a future
review. Any actual transition would separately explain governance, asset
and rights transfers, and handling of support funds; plans alone transfer
no existing rights.
